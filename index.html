Getting a clean read

  1. Face the light. A window or lamp in front of you beats one behind you.
  2. Fill the oval. Sit close enough that your face covers most of the guide.
  3. Park your eyes on the line. The cyan line marks where your eyes should sit; brows go above it.
  4. Hold still and calibrate. Neutral face, eyes open, three seconds. That becomes the baseline.
  5. Tune it. Watch the two bars — the pink marker is the trigger point. Slide it until a natural blink and a real brow lift cross it, and nothing else does.

Tracking reads the dark pixels around your eyes and brows, so glasses glare, a fringe over the brows, or a moving background will confuse it. Recalibrate whenever you shift position. Keyboard controls stay live the whole time: F or click to fire, Space to jump, P to pause.

Camera not starting? Browsers switch the camera off inside cross-origin iframes unless the embedding page explicitly passes it through, and a page cannot grant that to itself. Download this file and open it in Chrome or Firefox — or serve it from localhost — and the prompt will appear. Run the camera check for a readout of what your browser is doing.
